THE COMBO IS THE LIGHT
Everywhere else a combo pays out in points. Here it pays out in sight. The board is dark. Every tile you clear charges the lamp, and the lamp is the only way to see what you are clearing.
Tap a tile to break the run of three or more it belongs to. Tap a tile that is in no run and you have wasted a guess — three of those and it is over. A look costs about what one look earns back, so it only pays if you get several clears out of it. Columns that shift are marked: you learn what moved, never what it became.
