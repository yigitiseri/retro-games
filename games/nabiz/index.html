ONE STRIKE PER BEAT
Four drums, four speeds. Each one must be struck on the beat its light reaches the line — but you only get one strike per beat, and sooner or later two drums come due at once.
You can strike a drum early to clear it out of the way. It costs you almost nothing and pays almost nothing. Waiting for the line pays the drum's whole count, and the slow drums pay most. Miss one and it splits. Every pattern can be played clean — the cabinet checks before it deals one.
