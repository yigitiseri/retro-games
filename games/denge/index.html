IT BREAKS AT THE WEAKEST JOINT
The crate leaves the crane carrying the crane's speed, so dropping at the middle of the swing throws it furthest. Let go early and lead it.
A tower does not topple at its base. At every joint, the weight above has to sit over the crate below — so a crate placed badly ten floors down is a debt, and later crates have to go deliberately off-centre to pay it back. Stacking them straight is not the answer. Watch the gust, and watch the lean.
