ONE MORE FLIP?
Hold anywhere to spin. Let go and the rider straightens up — which takes time you may not have left.
You keep the flips you more than half finished, and landing them banks the lot. Every ramp is signed with the trick it will give you, and the sign never lies — but a gold sign means there is one more in it, if you can read the air. Land short of the far side and the run is over, so the speed you win from flips is the speed that clears the next gap: a bail costs you both.
