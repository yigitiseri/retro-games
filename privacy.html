Privacy

This is a hobby arcade of ten games. It is deliberately built so there is very little to say here, but here is all of it.

What stays on your device

Each game saves your best score and whether you left the sound on, using your browser's localStorage. That data never leaves your device, is never sent anywhere, and nobody but you can read it. Clearing your browser's site data for this site erases it.

Counting visits

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which tells me how many people visit and which games they open. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, it does not follow you to other websites, and it does not build a profile of you. That is why there is no cookie banner on this site: there is nothing to consent to storing.

Cloudflare receives the address of the page you opened, your browser type, your referrer if you came from a link, and your IP address — which it uses to work out an approximate country and then discards rather than retaining against you. Details are in Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation. I only ever see the totals, never anything about an individual visitor.

The coffee button

There is a “Buy me a coffee” link in the footer. Nothing is loaded from Ko-fi unless you click it. If you never click, your browser never contacts them at all. If you do click, a Ko-fi panel opens on the page and from that moment Ko-fi can see you, under their privacy policy — which is why it waits for you to ask rather than loading itself on every visit.

Everything else

Nothing: no advertising, no third-party trackers, no embedded videos, no social buttons. The two fonts the games use are served from this site itself rather than from Google Fonts, precisely so that loading a page does not hand your IP address to anyone else.

The one thing I cannot remove

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website anywhere, the server that sends you the page necessarily receives your IP address and your browser's user-agent string in order to answer the request. That is GitHub's processing, not mine, and it is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. I have no access to those logs and no way to switch them off.

Contact

The site and every line of its source are at github.com/yigitiseri/retro-games. Open an issue there if something here is wrong or unclear.

If this page ever stops being accurate — if analytics or anything else is added — it gets updated in the same change, not afterwards.
